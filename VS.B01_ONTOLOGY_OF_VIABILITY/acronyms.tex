This list records the abbreviations and structural codes used to identify
the project, its books, and its formal logical registers.

The entries are grouped according to their function. A structural or
registry code is not, by itself, a philosophical proposition, an ontological
commitment, or an indication of logical priority.

\section*{Editorial Note}

The codes listed above serve an organizational and referential function
within the philosophical library.

In particular, the distinction between an axiom, a definition, a
proposition, a thesis, a hypothesis, an argument, and a philosophical
foundation is a distinction between register categories. Their inclusion
in this list does not imply that all such categories possess the same
logical function, evidential status, or philosophical authority.

The classification of a particular entry must be determined by its
content, function, and formal evaluation within the relevant register.

Likewise, the abbreviation \textbf{VS} identifies the philosophical
system, while \textbf{B01} identifies the first book in the series.
Neither identifier should be treated as a substantive ontological claim.
