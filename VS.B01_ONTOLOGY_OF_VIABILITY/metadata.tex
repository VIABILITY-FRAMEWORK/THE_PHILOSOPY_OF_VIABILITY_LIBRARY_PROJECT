\newcommand{\BookDescription}{%
The first book of the Viability Philosophy System, establishing
an ontological framework for the philosophical system as a whole.
It investigates identity and manifestation, the realms of being,
ontological light, and the dynamics of being. Its purpose is to
clarify the fundamental ontological conditions, distinctions,
and relations required for the systematic articulation of
viability.%
