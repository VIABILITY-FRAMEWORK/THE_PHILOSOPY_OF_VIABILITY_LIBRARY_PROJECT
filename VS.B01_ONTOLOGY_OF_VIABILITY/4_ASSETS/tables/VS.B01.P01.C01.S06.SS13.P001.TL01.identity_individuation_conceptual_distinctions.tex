\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Conceptual Distinctions Relevant to Individuation}
\label{tab:identity-individuation-distinctions}

\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}

\begin{tabular}{|p{0.17\textwidth}|p{0.34\textwidth}|p{0.37\textwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{Concept}
&
\textbf{Central Question}
&
\textbf{What It Does Not Establish by Itself}
\\
\hline

Identity
&
What is the identity of this existent?
&
The ontological conditions through which it is constituted as an individual.
\\
\hline

Unity
&
Under what conditions does a configuration constitute a unity?
&
That the unity is necessarily one individual existent.
\\
\hline

Individuation
&
Under what conditions is an existent constituted as an individual?
&
That it possesses only one identity or remains numerically identical through every change.
\\
\hline

Distinction
&
How do existents or determinations differ or stand in relations of non-identity?
&
The ontological ground through which either existent is constituted as an individual.
\\
\hline

Singularity
&
In what respect is an existent singular within a specified context or domain?
&
A sufficient principle of individuation.
\\
\hline

Uniqueness
&
Which characteristics or configurations are not shared within a specified comparison?
&
The ontological constitution of the existent as an individual.
\\
\hline

Numerical Identity
&
Is an existent one and the same existent across a relevant comparison?
&
A complete account of the conditions through which it was constituted as an individual.
\\
\hline

Composition
&
How are components organized or combined within a configuration?
&
That the resulting configuration necessarily constitutes one individual.
\\
\hline

Relational Determination
&
How is an existent determined through its relations?
&
That its individuality is constituted entirely through those relations.
\\
\hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}
