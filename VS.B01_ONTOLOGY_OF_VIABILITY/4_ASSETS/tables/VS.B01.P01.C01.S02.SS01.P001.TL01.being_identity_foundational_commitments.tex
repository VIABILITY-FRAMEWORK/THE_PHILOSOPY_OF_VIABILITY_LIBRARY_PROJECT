\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{The Three Foundational Commitments Concerning Being and Identity}
\label{tab:being-identity-foundational-commitments}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\small

\begin{tabular}{|p{0.24\textwidth}|p{0.31\textwidth}|p{0.35\textwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{Commitment}
&
\textbf{Central Claim}
&
\textbf{What It Establishes}
\\
\hline

Analytical Distinction
&
Being and identity address distinguishable
conceptual questions.
&
Being concerns the actuality of what exists;
identity concerns the existent as the existent
that it is. Their conceptual distinction
does not establish ontological separation.
\\
\hline

Ontological Inseparability
&
Being and identity are not independently
established ontological actualities.
&
Neither is treated as an independently
existing component that can be detached
from the other. An existent is not first
actual in an identity-free condition.
\\
\hline

Absence of Ontological Priority
&
Neither being nor identity is ontologically
prior to the other.
&
Neither is regarded as the ontological
foundation from which the other is
subsequently produced, added, or derived.
\\
\hline

\end{tabular}

\end{table}
