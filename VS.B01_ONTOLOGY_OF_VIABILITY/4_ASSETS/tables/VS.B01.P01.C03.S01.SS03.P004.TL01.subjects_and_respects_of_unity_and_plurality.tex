\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}

\caption{Subjects and Respects of Unity and Plurality}
\label{tab:subjects-and-respects-of-unity-and-plurality}

\begin{tabular}{|p{2.4cm}|p{3.1cm}|p{3.2cm}|p{4.5cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Subject} &
\textbf{Possible Unity Claim} &
\textbf{Possible Plurality Claim} &
\textbf{Limit of Inference}
\\
\hline

Existent &
One existent considered as a referent or whole &
A plurality of distinct existents &
The unity attributed to an existent does not by itself determine the number of identities involved.
\\
\hline

Identity &
Unity attributed to an identity &
A plurality of identities &
Neither unity of an existent nor its internal differentiation settles the number of identities.
\\
\hline

Aggregate &
One collection under a criterion of membership &
A plurality of members &
The unity of a collection does not establish the unity of an identity.
\\
\hline

Composition &
One composed whole &
A plurality of components &
The number of components does not by itself determine the number of identities.
\\
\hline

Structure &
One organized configuration &
A plurality of organizational relations or elements &
Structural organization does not independently establish identity or numerical singularity.
\\
\hline

Relation &
A unified relational configuration &
A plurality of relations &
Several relations do not necessarily entail several existents or identities.
\\
\hline

Manifestation &
A particular manifested configuration or occurrence &
A plurality of manifestations &
The number of manifestations does not by itself determine the number of identities manifested.
\\
\hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}
