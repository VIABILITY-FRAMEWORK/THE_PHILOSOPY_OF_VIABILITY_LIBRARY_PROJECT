\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Conceptual Distinctions Concerning Numerical Identity}
\label{tab:numerical-identity-persistence-distinctions}

\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}

\begin{tabular}{|p{0.19\textwidth}|p{0.34\textwidth}|p{0.35\textwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{Concept}
&
\textbf{Central Question}
&
\textbf{What It Does Not Establish by Itself}
\\
\hline

Numerical Identity
&
Do different references concern one and the same existent?
&
The complete ontological constitution of that existent or the conditions of its persistence.
\\
\hline

Qualitative Sameness
&
Do existents share specified characteristics or determinations?
&
That they are numerically one and the same existent.
\\
\hline

Individuation
&
Under what conditions is an existent constituted as an individual?
&
That the individual persists as numerically the same existent under every transformation.
\\
\hline

Persistence
&
Under what conditions does an existent continue to exist?
&
That every form of continuity establishes numerical sameness.
\\
\hline

Material Continuity
&
Which material constituents remain or are replaced?
&
The numerical identity of the individual configuration.
\\
\hline

Structural Continuity
&
Which organizational relations or patterns are preserved?
&
The numerical identity of the existent instantiating the structure.
\\
\hline

Relational Continuity
&
Which relations involving an existent continue to obtain?
&
The persistence of the individual participating in those relations.
\\
\hline

Invariance
&
Which specified features remain preserved under a class of transformations?
&
A universal criterion of numerical identity.
\\
\hline

Signature
&
Through which configuration of determinations is an existent characterized?
&
The exhaustive ontological conditions of numerical sameness.
\\
\hline

Replacement
&
Has one individual ceased to exist and another taken its place?
&
That every qualitative, material, or structural change constitutes replacement.
\\
\hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}
