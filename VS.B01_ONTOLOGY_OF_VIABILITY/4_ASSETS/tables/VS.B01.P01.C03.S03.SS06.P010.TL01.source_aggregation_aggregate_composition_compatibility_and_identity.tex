\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}

\caption{Distinctions among Source, Aggregation, Aggregate,
Composition, Compatibility, and Identity}
\label{tab:source-aggregate-compatibility-and-identity}

\begin{tabular}{
    |p{0.17\linewidth}
    |p{0.35\linewidth}
    |p{0.35\linewidth}|
}
\hline
\textbf{Concept}
&
\textbf{Central concern}
&
\textbf{What it does not establish by itself}
\\
\hline

Source
&
A relation of origination, derivation, constitution,
or grounding between something and that in relation
to which it arises or is established.
&
That all entities related to the source possess
one identity, form one aggregate, or share every
determination.
\\
\hline

Unity of Source
&
The relation of multiple entities, manifestations,
or determinations to one and the same source
in a specified sense.
&
Unity of aggregate, numerical identity, or
the exclusive unity of identity.
\\
\hline

Aggregation
&
The bringing together, association, or grouping
of elements under a specified criterion or relation.
&
That the grouped elements form an integrated
composition or possess one identity.
\\
\hline

Aggregate
&
A plurality of elements considered together
as a collection, grouping, or bounded whole.
&
A common source, an integrated composition,
or unity of identity.
\\
\hline

Composition
&
The constitution and arrangement of components
in relation to a composed entity or configuration.
&
That the identity of the composed existent
is reducible to its components or their arrangement.
\\
\hline

Compatibility
&
The possibility of elements, determinations,
or conditions coexisting under specified
respects and conditions.
&
Actual aggregation, composition, common origin,
or unity of identity.
\\
\hline

Unity of Identity
&
The ontological unity attributed to an identity
as a distinct subject of inquiry.
&
It cannot be inferred solely from common source,
aggregate unity, compatibility, or compositional
organization.
\\
\hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}
