\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}

\caption{Unity of Identity and Related Forms of Unity}
\label{tab:unity-of-identity-and-related-forms-of-unity}

\begin{tabular}{|p{2.6cm}|p{3.0cm}|p{3.2cm}|p{4.1cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Form of Unity} &
\textbf{Primary Subject} &
\textbf{Question Addressed} &
\textbf{Limit of Inference}
\\
\hline

Unity of Identity &
Identity &
What ontological unity is attributable to identity? &
Does not, without further argument, establish that an existent involves exactly one identity.
\\
\hline

Numerical Unity &
A counted subject &
Is the subject counted as one under a criterion? &
Numerical counting does not by itself determine the ontological number of identities.
\\
\hline

Unity of Source &
A source and its related outcomes &
Are several existents or manifestations related to one source? &
A common source does not by itself establish one identity.
\\
\hline

Unity of Aggregate &
A collection &
Are multiple members collected under a criterion? &
A unified collection does not thereby constitute one identity.
\\
\hline

Unity of Composition &
A composed whole &
How do components participate in the constitution of a whole? &
Compositional integration does not independently determine identity.
\\
\hline

Structural Unity &
An organized configuration &
How are determinations or relations organized? &
Structural coherence does not by itself prove numerical or identity unity.
\\
\hline

Invariant Preservation &
A specified determination under transformation &
What remains preserved under specified conditions? &
Preservation does not independently establish unity of identity.
\\
\hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}
